# Dobble (Spot It!)

> A fast reflex game of pattern recognition and visual speed.

## Goal  

Be the fastest to spot the **one matching symbol** between two cards.

## Setup  

- 2–8 players  
- Shuffle the circular deck  
- Place cards according to the mini-game being played  

## Core Rule (Always True)  

- Any two cards share **exactly one common symbol**  
- The first player to identify it and say it aloud wins that interaction  

## Game Modes  

### 1. The Tower (Most Played)  

**Setup**  
- Give each player 1 card face down  
- Place the remaining cards as a draw pile in the center  

**Play**  
- Flip your card  
- Match your card with the top card of the pile  
- Say the matching symbol → take the pile card  

**Goal**  
- Collect the most cards  

### 2. The Well  

**Setup**  
- All cards are dealt equally among players  

**Play**  
- Players flip their top card simultaneously  
- Match your card with other players’ cards  
- Say the match → give your card to that player  

**Goal**  
- Get rid of all your cards first  

### 3. Hot Potato  

**Setup**  
- Each player starts with 1 card  

**Play**  
- Match your card with other players’ cards  
- When you find a match → give your card away  

**Goal**  
- Have the fewest cards at the end of the round  

### 4. Poisoned Gift  

**Setup**  
- Central pile + one card per player  

**Play**  
- Match your card with the center card  
- Say the match → give the center card to another player  

**Goal**  
- End with the fewest cards  

## Important Rules  

- You must say the symbol out loud  
- Only the **first correct call** counts  
- If incorrect → usually no penalty, but you lose time  

## Win Condition  

Depends on the mode being played:

- Collect the most cards  
- OR get rid of all cards  
- OR end with the fewest cards  

## Quick Strategy  

- Don’t scan randomly—look for **distinct shapes first**  
- Focus on **one card at a time**, not both equally  
- Practice improves recognition speed significantly  
- Stay calm—panic reduces accuracy
