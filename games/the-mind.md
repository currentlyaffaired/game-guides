# The Mind

> A silent cooperative game of timing, intuition, and shared rhythm.

## Goal  

Work together to play all cards in ascending order without talking or signaling.

## Setup  

- 2–4 players  
- Deck numbered 1 to 100  
- Start at Level 1  

Card distribution:
- Level 1 → 1 card per player  
- Level 2 → 2 cards per player  
- And so on (equal to level number)  

## How to Play  

### Round Flow  

- Each player looks at their own cards (keep them hidden)  
- Without speaking or signaling, players must play cards face up in the center  

### Core Rule  

- Cards must be played in **strict ascending order across all players**  

### Key Constraint  

- No talking  
- No planning  
- No gestures  
- Only timing and intuition are allowed  

### If a Mistake Happens  

- If a lower card is played after a higher one:  
  - All lower cards still in players’ hands are discarded  
  - The team loses **1 life**  

### Lives & Stars  

**Lives**  
- Represent how many mistakes the team can make  

**Throwing Star (Optional Ability)**  
- All players discard their **lowest card** face up  
- Helps reset difficult situations  

### Progression  

- Successfully complete a level → move to the next level  
- Each level increases difficulty (more cards per player)  

## End of Game  

The game ends when:
- All levels are completed, OR  
- All lives are lost  

## Win Condition  

Complete the final level (depends on player count).

## Losing Condition  

Lose all lives before completing the required levels.

## Easy Way to Remember  

Think silently → Play in order → Trust timing

## Strategy  

- Use time gaps:
  - Smaller numbers → play sooner  
  - Larger numbers → wait longer  
- Develop a shared rhythm over multiple rounds  
- Avoid rushing—mistakes cost lives  
- Use stars strategically in high-pressure situations
