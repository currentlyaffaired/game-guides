# Codenames

> A word-association game of clues and deduction.

## Goal  
Identify all your team’s words before the opponent.

## Setup  
- Split into 2 teams  
- Assign spymasters  
- Lay out 5×5 grid  
- Spymasters get key card
- One team starts which has one extra word

### Roles
Spymaster
- Knows the identity of all words
- Gives clues to help teammates guess correctly

Operatives
- Try to guess the correct words based on the clue

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
1. Spymaster gives clue
- Format: one word + one number
- Example: “Ocean 2”
2. Team (Operative) guesses
- They can make up to number + 1 guesses
- They point to words on the grid
3. Reveal results
- If correct → stays revealed, continue guessing
- If wrong → turn ends immediately

### Word Types  
- Team words → correct guesses
- Opponent words → helps the other team
- Neutral → harmless but ends turn
- Assassin → instant loss if chosen

### Clue Rules  
- One word only  
- No direct hints  
- Must relate to meaning
- No gestures, spelling hints, or extra info

## End of Game  
Game ends when a team finishes or assassin is chosen.

## Win Condition  
Reveal all your words first.

## Quick Mental Model  
Clue → Guess → Avoid assassin

## Strategy  
- Use safe connections  
- Avoid overguessing  
- Track revealed info
