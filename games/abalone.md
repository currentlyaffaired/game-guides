# Abalone

> A strategic marble game of positioning and pushing.

## Goal  
Push 6 opponent marbles off the board.

## Setup  
- Each player gets 14 marbles  
- Arrange in starting formation  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
- Move 1–3 marbles in a straight line  

### Types of Moves  

**Inline Move**
- Move 1–3 marbles in a straight line
- Can push opponent marbles if you have more in line
- Cannot push your own marbles

**Broadside Move**
- Move a group of 2–3 marbles sideways
- Cannot push  
- Used for positioning

### Sumito Rule  
- 3 pushes 2  
- 2 pushes 1  
- Equal cannot push
- And path ahead is clear or leads off the board

## End of Game  
When 6 marbles are pushed out.

## Win Condition  
First to remove 6 wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Align → Push → Control

## Strategy  
- Stay grouped  
- Control center  
- Avoid edges
