# Flip 7

> A push-your-luck card game about risk and timing.

## Goal  
Be the first to reach the target score (usually 200).

## Setup  
- Shuffle the deck  
- All players start at 0 points
- Choose a starting player

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
- - Flip the top card and add it to your row
- Choose: stop (bank your points) or continue (risk it)

### Core Rule - The Catch
- If you flip a duplicate number → you bust and score 0  

### Number Cards
- Each card has a value
- All values you collect = your score for the round

### Special Cards  
- Freeze → Immediately stop and lock in your points
- Flip Three → flip 3 cards at once (high risk, high reward)
- Second Chance → ignore one duplicate (saves from busting)
- x2 Multiplier → doubles your total round score 

## End of Round  
- Players bank points or bust  
- Scores are added
- Start a new round

## Win Condition  
First to target score wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Flip → Risk → Stop

## Strategy  
- Play aggressive early  
- Bank points when ahead  
- Use safety cards to push limits
