# Secret Hitler

> A social deduction game of hidden roles, political maneuvering, and deception.

## Goal  

Depends on your hidden role:

- **Liberals** → Pass 5 Liberal policies OR eliminate Hitler  
- **Fascists** → Pass 6 Fascist policies OR get Hitler elected as Chancellor (after 3 Fascist policies are passed)  

## Setup  

- 5–10 players  
- Each player receives a secret role card:
  - Liberals (majority)  
  - Fascists (minority)  
  - Hitler (special Fascist role)  

- Fascists know each other  
- Hitler may or may not know the Fascists (depends on player count)  

- Shuffle the policy deck (Liberal + Fascist cards)  

## How to Play  

### Game Flow (Each Round)

### 1. Elect Government  

- The President nominates a Chancellor  
- All players vote (Ja / Nein)  
- If the majority approves → the government is formed  

### 2. Legislative Session  

- President draws 3 policy cards and discards 1  
- Passes 2 cards to the Chancellor  
- Chancellor discards 1 and enacts the remaining policy  

### 3. Execute Policy Effect  

- Place the enacted policy on the board (Liberal or Fascist)  
- Some Fascist policies unlock special powers  

## Fascist Powers (As Game Progresses)  

Depending on how many Fascist policies have been passed, powers may include:

- Peek at top policy cards  
- Investigate a player’s loyalty  
- Special election  
- Execution (eliminate a player)  

## Key Rule (Hitler Condition)  

- After **3 Fascist policies** are enacted:  
  - If Hitler is elected as Chancellor → **Fascists win instantly**  

## Election Tracker (Failed Votes)  

- If **3 governments fail in a row**:
  - The top policy card is enacted automatically  
  - The election tracker resets  

## End of Game  

The game ends immediately when a win condition is met.

## Win Condition  

- **Liberals:**  
  - Pass 5 Liberal policies OR eliminate Hitler  

- **Fascists:**  
  - Pass 6 Fascist policies OR elect Hitler as Chancellor (after 3 Fascist policies)  

## Easy Way to Remember  

Elect → Pass policies → Build trust or deception → Trigger win condition

## Strategy  

### As Liberal  
- Track voting patterns and policy outcomes carefully  
- Don’t trust early claims too easily  
- In the late game, control Chancellor selections  

### As Fascist  
- Blend in—avoid overplaying your role  
- Create confusion among Liberals  
- Set up Hitler for a safe election after 3 Fascist policies  

### As Hitler  
- Stay low-profile early  
- Avoid drawing suspicion  
- Position yourself for a mid-game election win
