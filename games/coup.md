# Coup

> A fast bluffing game of power, deception, and elimination.

## Goal  
Be the last player with at least one influence (card) remaining.

## Setup  
- Each player gets 2 cards face down (influences)  
- Each player starts with 2 coins  
- Place remaining cards as a deck  
- Keep coins in a common supply  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
On your turn, choose ONE action:

### Basic Actions (Always Allowed)  
- Income → Take 1 coin  
- Foreign Aid → Take 2 coins (can be blocked by Duke)  
- Coup (cost: 7 coins) → Force a player to lose 1 influence (cannot be blocked)  

### Character Actions (Bluffing Allowed)  
You can claim ANY role:

- Duke → Take 3 coins  
- Assassin (cost: 3 coins) → Make a player lose 1 influence  
- Captain → Steal 2 coins  
- Ambassador → Draw and choose cards  
- Contessa → Blocks assassination  

### Challenges & Blocking  

**Challenge:**  
- Call a bluff  
- If wrong → you lose a card  
- If correct → opponent loses a card  

**Blocking:**  
- Foreign Aid → Duke  
- Assassin → Contessa  
- Steal → Captain/Ambassador
You can challenge a block too.

### Losing Influence
- When you lose a challenge or get targeted → reveal 1 card
- Revealed cards are out of the game
- Lose both = you’re out

## Important Rules
- If you have 10+ coins, you must Coup
- You can bluff anytime — that’s the core of the game
- Information = power

## End of Game  
Game continues until only one player has influence.

## Win Condition  
Last player remaining wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Bluff → Challenge → Eliminate

## Strategy  
- Bluff selectively  
- Track coins constantly  
- Force difficult decisions
