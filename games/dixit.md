# Dixit

> A creative storytelling game of imagination and interpretation.

## Goal  
Score points by giving and guessing clues.

## Setup  
- Each player gets 6 cards  
- Provide voting tokens  
- Choose storyteller  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
1. Storyteller gives a clue
- word, phrase, sentence, sound—anything (but no pointing or inside signals)
2. Others submit matching cards they think that could fit the clue
3. Shuffle and reveal
- Lay out all cards face up
4. Players vote
- Players (except storyteller) vote on which card they think is the storyteller’s

### Scoring Rules  

**If all or none guess correctly:**  
- Storyteller: 0  
- Others: 2  

**If some guess correctly:**  
- Storyteller: 3  
- Correct guessers: 3  

**Bonus:**  
- +1 per vote received  

## End of Round  
- Draw back to 6 cards  
- Rotate storyteller  

## Win Condition  
First to 30 points wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Clue → Mix → Guess

## Strategy  
- Aim for “some but not all”  
- Avoid obvious clues  
- Mislead subtly
