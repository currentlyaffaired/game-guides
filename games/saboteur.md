# Saboteur

> A hidden-role card game of cooperation, deception, and disruption.

## Goal  

Depends on your hidden role:

- **Miners** → Build a path to reach the hidden gold  
- **Saboteurs** → Prevent miners from reaching the gold  

## Setup  

- 3–10 players  
- Deal each player a secret role card (Miner or Saboteur)  

Place on the table:
- Start card  
- 3 Goal cards (1 gold, 2 coal), placed face down  

- Shuffle and deal hand cards to all players  

## Card Types  

### Path Cards  
- Used to build tunnels from the start toward the goal cards  
- Paths must connect logically  

### Action Cards  

- **Break Tools** → Block a player from building  
- **Repair Tools** → Fix broken tools  
- **Map** → Peek at a goal card  
- **Rockfall** → Remove a path card from play  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  

On your turn, choose ONE action:

#### 1. Play a Path Card  
- Extend the tunnel  
- Must connect correctly with existing paths  

#### 2. Play an Action Card  
- Can help teammates or hinder others  

#### 3. Discard a Card  
- If no useful move is available  

After your action:  
- Draw a card (if the deck is not empty)  

### Blocked Players  

- If your tool is broken:  
  - You **cannot play path cards**  
  - You must be repaired before building again  

## End of Round  

The round ends when:

- A path successfully reaches the **gold** → **Miners win**  
- OR no more moves are possible → **Saboteurs win**  

## Scoring  

- If **Miners win** → miners share gold cards (points)  
- If **Saboteurs win** → saboteurs receive gold instead  

- The game is typically played over multiple rounds  

## End of Game  

After all rounds are completed:

- Players total their collected gold  

## Win Condition  

The player with the **highest total gold** wins.

## Easy Way to Remember  

Build path → Help or sabotage → Reach gold or block it

## Strategy  

### As Miner  
- Don’t trust blindly—watch who delays progress  
- Use map cards wisely to confirm gold  
- Repair teammates strategically  

### As Saboteur  
- Be subtle—avoid obvious sabotage early  
- Create inefficient paths rather than blocking directly  
- Time your disruption near the end
