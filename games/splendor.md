# Splendor

> An engine-building game of resource management and efficient card acquisition.

## Goal  

Be the first player to reach **15 prestige points**.

## Setup  

- 2–4 players  
- Lay out 3 tiers of development cards:
  - Level 1  
  - Level 2  
  - Level 3  
- Reveal 4 cards per tier  
- Place gem tokens (5 colors + gold/joker)  
- Place Noble tiles based on player count  

## How to Play  

On your turn, choose ONE of the following actions:

### 1. Take Gems  

- Take **3 different colors**, OR  
- Take **2 of the same color** (only if 4 or more of that color are available)  

### 2. Reserve a Card  

- Take any visible card OR the top card of a deck into your hand  
- Take **1 gold token** (wild/joker)  
- You may have a maximum of **3 reserved cards**  

### 3. Buy a Card  

- Pay the required gems  
- Add the card to your tableau  

You gain:
- A **permanent gem bonus** (discount for future purchases)  
- **Prestige points** (if the card has any)  

## Core Mechanics  

### Engine Building  
- Cards provide permanent discounts for future purchases  
- These bonuses stack and make later cards easier to buy  

### Nobles (Bonus Points)  

- Automatically claimed when you meet their requirements  
- Do not require an action  
- Typically worth **3 prestige points**  

## Important Rules  

- Maximum **10 tokens** in hand (discard excess if needed)  
- Bonuses from cards permanently reduce costs  
- Gold tokens act as **wildcards**  

## End of Game  

- Triggered when a player reaches **15 or more points**  
- The round continues until all players have had equal turns  
- Final scores are then compared  

## Win Condition  

- The player with the **highest total prestige points** wins  

## Easy Way to Remember  

Take gems → Buy cards → Build discounts → Score

## Strategy  

- Early: focus on cheap cards that provide bonuses  
- Mid game: chain purchases using your discount engine  
- Avoid hoarding tokens—convert them into cards quickly  
- Track opponents’ plans, especially noble requirements  
- Level 3 cards are powerful but require setup—don’t rush blindly
