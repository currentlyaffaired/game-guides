# Cricket Champions

> A simplified cricket simulation focused on scoring and decision-making.

## Goal  
Score more runs than your opponent.

## Setup  
- 2 players or 2 teams
- Choose overs  
- Assign batting and bowling  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
- Batting plays shots  
- Bowling responds  

### Core Mechanics  
- Outcomes: runs, dot balls, wickets  
- Overs limit gameplay  
- Wickets reduce scoring ability  

## End of Game  
After all overs are completed.

## Win Condition  
Highest runs wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Score → Defend → Chase

## Strategy  
- Balance risk  
- Maintain run rate  
- Avoid early wickets
