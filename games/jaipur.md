# Jaipur

> A two-player trading game of timing and efficiency.

## Goal  
Earn more points across 2 or 3 rounds (best of three).

## Setup
- Shuffle goods tokens into stacks
- Create market with 5 cards
- Include three camels → kept separately (not in hand)
- Deal 5 cards to each player  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
Choose ONE:

1. Take cards  
2. Exchange cards  
3. Sell goods  

### Core Rules  

**Taking Cards**
- Take one card 
- OR all camels
- OR exchange multiple cards (trade equal number with market)

**Selling**
- Sell one type  
- High-value goods require ≥2 cards
- Take tokens equal to number of cards sold
- Tokens decrease in value as the game progresses

**Bonuses**
- Selling 3+ gives bonus tokens  

### Camels  
- Used for trading  
- Bonus for most camels at end

### Hand Limit
- Maximum 7 cards in hand (camels don’t count)

## End of Round  
- Ends when tokens or deck runs out  
- Count points  
- Award seal  

## Win Condition  
Win 2 rounds.

## Quick Mental Model  
Take → Trade → Sell

## Strategy  
- Time large sales  
- Control market  
- Use camels for tempo
