# Taboo

> A fast-paced word game of description under restriction.

## Goal  
Get your team to guess as many words as possible without using forbidden words.

## Setup  
- Split into 2 teams  
- Set a timer (~60 seconds)  
- One player is the clue giver  
- Opponents monitor for rule violations  

## How to Play  

### Turn Structure  
1. Start timer  
2. Clue giver describes word  
3. Team guesses freely  
4. Move to next card on correct guess  

### Core Rules  
- You cannot use taboo words listed on the card  
- No variations (plural, tense, rhyming words, etc.)  
- No gestures or spelling hints

### Allowed:
- Synonyms, descriptions, examples
- Sounds, emotions, contexts

### Violations
- Opponents call out violations  
- Card is skipped or penalized  

## End of Round  
- Count correct guesses
- Teams switch roles  

## Win Condition  
Team with highest score wins.

## Quick Mental Model  
Describe → Avoid → Guess fast

## Strategy  
- Skip quickly if stuck  
- Start broad, then narrow  
- Focus on speed over perfection
