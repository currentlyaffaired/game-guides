# Avalon

> A hidden-role game of deduction, trust, and deception centered around secret teams and quests.

## Goal  

Two sides with hidden roles:

- **Good (Arthur’s side)** → Complete 3 successful quests  
- **Evil (Mordred’s side)** → Fail 3 quests OR assassinate Merlin at the end  

## Setup  

- 5–10 players  
- Each player gets a secret role  

### Good Roles  
- **Merlin** (knows evil players, with limitations)  
- **Percival** (knows Merlin candidates)  
- **Loyal Servants of Arthur**  

### Evil Roles  
- **Assassin**  
- **Morgana**  
- **Mordred** (hidden from Merlin)  
- **Oberon** (hidden from the evil team)  

- Assign roles based on player count  
- Decide the first leader  

## How to Play  

### Game Flow (Each Round)

### 1. Team Proposal  
- The current leader proposes a team for the quest  

### 2. Vote  
- All players vote to approve or reject the team  
- If **majority approves** → quest proceeds  
- If **rejected** → next player becomes leader and proposes a new team  
- If **5 consecutive team proposals are rejected** → **Evil wins immediately**  

### 3. Quest Phase  
- Selected players secretly submit one card each:  
  - **Success** (can be played by anyone)  
  - **Fail** (can only be played by Evil players)  

- All cards are shuffled and revealed  

### 4. Quest Result  
- If **any Fail card is revealed** → quest fails  
- If **all cards are Success** → quest succeeds  

*(Note: In some player counts, later quests may require more than one Fail card to fail, depending on official rules.)*

## Endgame (Critical Twist)

If **Good completes 3 successful quests**:

- Evil gets one final chance to win  
- The **Assassin guesses who Merlin is**  

  - If the guess is **correct** → Evil wins  
  - If the guess is **wrong** → Good wins  

## End of Game  

The game ends immediately when:
- Good completes 3 successful quests **and assassination is resolved**, OR  
- Evil fails 3 quests, OR  
- 5 consecutive team votes fail  

## Win Condition  

- **Good:**  
  - Complete 3 successful quests  
  - Merlin is NOT correctly identified  

- **Evil:**  
  - Fail 3 quests  
  - OR correctly assassinate Merlin  
  - OR force 5 failed team votes  

## Easy Way to Remember  

Propose team → Vote → Secret outcome → Repeat → Assassin decides

## Strategy  

### As Good  
- Track **voting consistency** and **quest outcomes**  
- Avoid blindly trusting early leaders  
- Protect Merlin—avoid making their identity obvious  

### As Merlin  
- Guide decisions subtly without exposing yourself  
- Avoid being too accurate too early (draws suspicion)  

### As Evil  
- Mix **Success and Fail cards strategically**  
- Build trust before sabotaging  
- Create confusion around Merlin’s identity  

### As Assassin  
- Observe who appears **too informed or confident**  
- Save your final judgment carefully for the endgame
