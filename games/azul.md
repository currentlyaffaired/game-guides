# Azul

> A tile-drafting game of pattern building, efficiency, and timing.

## Goal  

Score the most points by placing tiles to complete rows and patterns on your wall.

## Setup  

- 2–4 players  
- Each player gets a player board  
- Place factory displays (5, 7, or 9 depending on player count)  
- Fill each factory with 4 tiles from the bag  
- Place the first player marker in the center  

## How to Play  

Each round consists of 3 phases:

### 1. Factory Offer Phase  

On your turn:

- Choose a factory display OR the center  
- Take **all tiles of one color** from that source  

Then:

- If taken from a factory → remaining tiles move to the center  
- If taken from the center → you also take the **first player marker** (for next round)  

- Place the taken tiles into one of your **pattern lines**  

#### Placement Rules  

- Each pattern line can hold **only one color**  
- The capacity of each line equals its row number (1 to 5 tiles)  
- If you take more tiles than the line can hold → extra tiles go to the floor line (penalty risk)  

### 2. Wall Tiling Phase (After All Tiles Are Taken)  

- Move **one tile** from each **completed pattern line** to your wall  
- Place it in the correct position (based on the board’s color pattern)  
- Score points immediately  

#### Scoring Basics  

- A single tile placed alone = **1 point**  
- Adjacent tiles (horizontal and/or vertical) increase points  
- Larger connected groups = higher scoring  

### 3. Floor Line (Penalties)  

- Extra or invalid tiles go to your **floor line**  
- At the end of the round → lose points based on the number of tiles in the floor line  

## End of Round  

- Refill all factory displays  
- Start the next round  
- The player who took the **first player marker** goes first  

## End of Game  

The game ends when:

- A player completes a **full horizontal row** on their wall  

Final scoring:

- Completed rows, columns, and full color sets grant **bonus points**  

## Win Condition  

The player with the **highest total score** wins.

## Easy Way to Remember  

Take tiles → Fill lines → Move to wall → Score → Avoid penalties

## Strategy  

- Prioritize completing lines every round (tempo matters)  
- Avoid taking tiles that cause heavy floor penalties  
- Deny opponents key colors when possible  
- In mid/late game: focus on row, column, and color-set bonuses—not just immediate points
