# Mafia

> A social deduction game of deception, discussion, and elimination.

## Goal  

Two opposing sides:

- **Mafia** → Eliminate enough players to equal or outnumber the town  
- **Town (Civilians)** → Identify and eliminate all Mafia members  

## Setup  

- 5+ players (works best with 7–12)  
- One player is the **Moderator** (not part of the game)  

Assign secret roles randomly:
- Mafia (minority)  
- Town (majority)  
- Optional special roles (Doctor, Detective, etc.)  

## Game Structure  

The game alternates between **Night** and **Day** phases.

## How to Play  

### Night Phase (Eyes Closed)  

The Moderator calls roles one by one:

- **Mafia** → secretly choose a player to eliminate  
- **Doctor (optional)** → chooses a player to save  
- **Detective (optional)** → investigates a player’s role  

- All actions are resolved silently  

### Day Phase (Discussion & Voting)  

- Moderator reveals who was eliminated (if any)  
- All players discuss openly  
- Players may accuse, defend, and bluff  

Then:

- Players vote to eliminate one player (lynching)  
- The player with the majority votes is eliminated  

### Elimination Rules  

- Eliminated players must reveal their role  
- They are out of the game and cannot speak further  

## Common Roles  

### Mafia  
- Know each other  
- Coordinate eliminations  

### Town (Civilians)  
- No special information  
- Use logic and behavior to identify Mafia  

### Doctor  
- Can save one player each night  

### Detective  
- Learns whether a player is Mafia or not each night  

## End of Game  

The game ends when one faction meets its win condition.

## Win Condition  

- **Mafia:** Equal or outnumber the town  
- **Town:** Eliminate all Mafia members  

## Easy Way to Remember  

Night → Mafia act → Day → Discuss → Vote → Repeat

## Strategy  

### As Town  
- Focus on voting patterns, not just what people say  
- Watch for inconsistencies across multiple rounds  
- Avoid tunnel vision—re-evaluate constantly  

### As Mafia  
- Blend in—avoid being too quiet or too aggressive  
- Create doubt between town members  
- Control the narrative during discussions
